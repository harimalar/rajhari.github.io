Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026

Publisher and contact

Annam Care is published by Malar Hari. For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to support, email rajhari@gmail.com. The publisher cannot remotely access, recover or erase records stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

Annam Care stores the name and preferences you enter, food and water logs, favorite foods, Quick Plates, herbal intake and fasting records to provide your journal, summaries and reminders. Glucose, lipid, medicine, taken-dose and Medical ID records are stored in the private vault. Herbal and supplement intake is stored in the ordinary journal, outside the authenticated vault. Annam has no account, developer-run cloud database, analytics, advertising or network AI service. Typed food matching runs on this device.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Steps and active energy are read to display your movement. If you choose glucose import from the unlocked vault, imported readings are stored there as local historical copies. Turn off sync in Annam and manage read permissions in Apple Health. Annam does not write to Apple Health or use Health data for advertising. Imported glucose is a historical copy; later changes or deletions in Apple Health are not automatically mirrored in the vault. Erasing Annam records does not erase Apple Health records.

Voice entry

Voice entry requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. Recognition uses the device when supported; otherwise audio is processed by Apple Speech and may be sent to Apple servers. Annam does not save audio recordings. Review the transcript before saving a meal. You can type instead and revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Widgets, reminders and sharing

Widgets receive opted-in daily calorie and water totals, goals and display units. These may be visible on the Home or Lock Screen. Turning sharing off removes the shared snapshot and requests a widget refresh; iOS may temporarily retain an already rendered widget. Private readings, medicines and doses are never shared with widgets. iOS schedules local reminders, which may appear on the Lock Screen. Care reminders use generic text without medicine names or doses, but their timing may reveal that a reminder exists. Reports and fasting backups are shared only when you choose an export or share destination; those files may contain sensitive records. The receiving app or service handles its copy under its own privacy practices.

Storage and retention

Journal and fasting files use iOS file protection. Vault records use AES-GCM encryption with a device-only Keychain key requiring user presence and a device passcode. Leaving the vault or backgrounding the app clears its unlocked session. Records remain locally until you delete them or remove the app. Keychain items can survive app removal, so erase private records and disable Medical ID sharing before uninstalling if you want to remove those previews. Annam hides orphaned previews when no vault file is present. Record files are excluded from automatic device backups; app preferences may be backed up by iOS. Keep original lab reports and prescriptions. Losing the device, removing the app or removing the passcode can make vault records unrecoverable.

Medical ID and locked-vault summaries

Medical ID is private by default and requires vault authentication to edit. If you turn on Show while vault is locked and save, a separate device-only Keychain copy of your entered blood type, allergies and emergency contact becomes readable by anyone using this unlocked device, without vault authentication. It is not shown on the iOS Lock Screen. Disable the option and save, delete Medical ID, or erase private health records to remove that copy. Lab-result counts, the latest lab date and the most recent glucose import date are also cached in device-only Keychain storage for the Private tab; they contain no readings or medicine details. These previews do not sync to iCloud, appear in widgets, or bypass the device passcode. The app ignores orphaned previews if its encrypted vault file is absent.

Your controls

You can delete individual records. Erase food and water journal deletes meal and water entries only; it leaves favorites, Quick Plates, herbal intake, fasting history and private vault records unchanged. Manage fasting history in Fasting, herbal intake in Herbs & Supplements, and private health records from the unlocked vault. Delete any exported copies separately from their destinations. Annam cannot retrieve or delete copies you shared elsewhere. Clear your profile name in You; remove saved plates and favorites in Food. You can revoke microphone, speech and notification permissions in iOS Settings and Health permissions in Apple Health.

Device and external services

Protect your device and passcode. Annam cannot prevent access to an already unlocked screen, screenshots or copies you choose to share. Opening external links uses your browser or another app and is subject to that service’s policies. Apple Speech and Apple Health are Apple services.

Support communications and diagnostics

If you choose to email support, your email provider delivers the message and any attachments to the publisher. Include only what is needed; do not send health records, Medical ID details or passwords. Support messages are used to respond to your request; you can ask for their deletion, subject to necessary legal or security retention. Annam adds no logs or attachments automatically. Apple may process App Store and device diagnostics under your system settings; Annam embeds no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children and changes

Annam is not designed for children. Fasting and diet targets are not recommendations for children or adolescents. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact the publisher. Material changes to data use will be described in an updated policy; any new optional access will require an explicit choice. The date above identifies this version.
