Privacy Policy

ChittuPetti · Effective November 11, 2026

ChittuPetti is designed to keep receipt handling on your Apple device. This policy describes the current version of the app and will be updated if its data practices change.

Data collection

ChittuPetti does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. Receipt recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework.

Information stored on your device

When you scan or import receipts, ChittuPetti stores the PDFs and associated details in local app storage. These can include recognized text, vendor, purchase date, amount, currency, review status, category, and app preferences. Camera, Photos, and Files access occurs only when you choose the related action.

Files, backups, and sharing

Filed PDFs are also available in Files under On My iPhone → ChittuPetti → Receipts. Apple device backups follow the settings you choose with Apple. If you export or share a PDF or ZIP, the destination you select handles that copy under its own terms and privacy practices. Copies previously exported or shared are outside the app’s control.

App lock

The optional Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode lock gates the app interface. It does not encrypt PDFs or protect files visible in Files, device backups, or exported copies.

Retention and deletion

Receipts remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app’s stored files. Deleting a receipt removes the copies managed by ChittuPetti, but cannot remove copies you have exported or shared. Keep independent backups of records you need.

Children

ChittuPetti is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when it does. For privacy questions, contact rajhari@gmail.com.
