# ScreenNestly Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Publisher: Hari Rajagopalan · Contact: [rajhari@gmail.com](mailto:rajhari@gmail.com)

## A local screenshot app

ScreenNestly has no cloud service, account system, hosted sharing, advertising, or analytics. The app does not upload your captures, recognized text, file paths, or usage history to the developer. We do not sell your information. Capture, editing, organization, and text recognition work on your Mac.

This policy covers the ScreenNestly macOS app. Visiting the documentation website or contacting support uses separate services described below.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app keeps screenshot originals, edited PNGs, editable annotations, recognized text for search, capture names and dates, favorites, and project associations in its local application container. Preferences, shortcuts, folder-access bookmarks, organization rules, and local file-operation history support the features you enable. There is no developer-operated copy of your library or remote recovery service.

OCR uses Apple's Vision framework on your device. Images and recognized text are not sent to an online AI service. Editing retains the original locally so you can revise or undo changes. Copy, drag, and image export use the current saved rendered image; sharing the app's underlying storage may expose originals and recognized text.

## Permissions and their purposes

- **Screen Recording / Screen & System Audio Recording:** lets ScreenNestly capture a selected area, window, display, or scrolling sequence. Despite the system category's name, ScreenNestly captures still images and does not record audio. Capture starts when you request it; scrolling capture continues until you finish, cancel, or a limit stops it.
- **Accessibility / event posting:** optional access lets Auto-Scroll send scrolling gestures to the selected application. Manual scrolling works without this access. Global shortcuts use macOS hotkey registration; they do not require general keystroke monitoring.
- **Files and folders:** user-selected folder access lets Auto-Rules watch, move, and rename matching images, and lets projects receive image exports. Folder watching is optional and can remain active while the menu-bar app is running.
- **Notifications and login item:** optional settings provide local organization notifications and launch the app when you sign in.

You can decline optional permissions and change macOS permissions in System Settings. Screen capture requires Screen Recording access; importing existing images does not. Turn off Auto-Rules to stop watching. Quit ScreenNestly to stop its active capture and watching sessions. Disable launch at login in the app or macOS Login Items settings.

## Sharing, exports, and backups

Copying places an image or recognized text on the macOS clipboard. Dragging and exporting provide copies to the destination you choose. Other apps, Universal Clipboard, and any synchronization or backup software you have configured handle those copies under their own settings and policies. Selecting a folder managed by another service does not add a ScreenNestly cloud feature, but that service may transfer files placed there, including files moved by Auto-Rules.

Drag operations create temporary PNG copies so the receiving app can finish reading them. ScreenNestly attempts to remove its drag-export folders older than one day when another drag export is created; this is not immediate deletion. macOS or receiving apps may also retain transfer caches.

**Save Backup exports organization rules and settings only. It does not back up your screenshot library, image originals, edited images, or project-folder contents.** Use your own filesystem backup, such as Time Machine, for those files. Exported settings backups can include paths and folder-access bookmarks; share them only with people you trust.

## Retention and deletion

Library records remain until you delete them. Delete moves the app's owned original and edited files to macOS Trash and removes the library record and its search text. Project exports, copies shared elsewhere, clipboard contents, transfer caches, and your backups are separate; delete those at their destinations if needed. Trash remains recoverable until emptied through macOS. Removing the app alone may leave its container behind.

Reset App Settings clears organization rules and local operation history; it is not an erase-library command and retains the selected screenshot-folder access. ScreenNestly cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local files. Contact support for guidance before manually removing app data.

## Support, diagnostics, and the website

If you email support, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to answer your request, investigate defects, or address security matters. Do not include passwords or private captures. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to necessary legal or security retention. ScreenNestly attaches nothing automatically.

The app includes no analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. macOS may keep local diagnostic logs, and Apple may process diagnostics or purchase information according to your Apple settings and its policies. The developer does not receive your payment-card details from the app.

The documentation is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening website links makes ordinary browser requests to GitHub; its hosting practices are covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The ScreenNestly pages add no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Apple services are governed by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
