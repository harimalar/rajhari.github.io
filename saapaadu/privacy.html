தனியுரிமைக் கொள்கை

புதுப்பிக்கப்பட்ட நாள் · Updated: 25 November 2026

சாப்பாட்டில் உங்கள் பயன்பாட்டுத் தரவு சாதனத்திலேயே சேமிக்கப்படுகிறது. தனியான கணக்கு, விளம்பரம், பகுப்பாய்வு சேவை அல்லது உருவாக்குநரின் சேவையகத்திற்கு தானியங்கித் தரவு அனுப்புதல் இல்லை.

பிடித்தவை, உணவுத் திட்டங்கள், தட்டு, மளிகை, வீட்டிலுள்ள பொருட்கள், குறிப்புகள், சமையல் முன்னேற்றம், சமீபத்தில் சமைத்தவை, டைமர் மற்றும் தோற்ற விருப்பங்கள் உங்கள் iPhone-இல் இருக்கும். மளிகைப் பட்டியலைப் பகிர்வதும் ஆதரவுக்கு மின்னஞ்சல் அனுப்புவதும் உங்கள் தேர்வு.

Data handled by the app

Saapaadu does not collect app usage data or send your saved cooking information to the developer. The app has no account service, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend.

Favourites, dated meal plans, your plate, grocery items, pantry selections, personal recipe notes, cooking progress, recent cooking history, timer state and appearance preferences are saved locally on your device. The app does not request access to HealthKit, contacts, location, microphone, camera or your photo library.

Notifications

Cooking timers may use local notifications with your permission. No remote push service or push token is used by Saapaadu. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Sharing and support

If you choose Share, iOS sends the grocery-list text to the app or recipient you select. Saapaadu does not choose a recipient or send it automatically. The receiving service handles the information under its own privacy practices.

If you email support, your email address and the contents you choose to send are received at rajhari@gmail.com for responding to your request. App records are not automatically attached. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. You may ask us to delete a support email you sent, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

External links and this website

Opening support, privacy or reference links uses your browser and an internet connection. Those sites may process ordinary connection information under their own policies. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no added analytics or tracking scripts. See GitHub’s privacy statement for hosting-related processing.

Control, backups and deletion

You can edit or remove saved items using the corresponding app controls. Removing the app through iOS deletes its local app data; offloading is different and retains documents and data. iPhone backups or device migration may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Saapaadu has no separate cloud sync or backup service. Manage Apple backups through your device or Apple account.

Children and changes

Saapaadu does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated.
