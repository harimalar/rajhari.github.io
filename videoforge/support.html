Tell us what happened

Include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and what you expected. A screenshot with private information removed can help. Attach a short sample only if needed and you have permission to share it. Do not send passwords, cloud credentials, passcodes, full photo libraries, or sensitive personal footage. The app never sends a report automatically.

Find pCloud or NAS files

Open the provider’s app and sign in. In Files → Browse → … → Edit, enable the provider. For SMB/NAS, use Files → Browse → … → Connect to Server and connect on a reachable network. In VideoForge choose Files, Cloud & NAS. iOS may reopen the last visited folder; use Browse or the back arrow to reach Locations. VideoForge cannot sign in to or repair an external provider on your behalf.

Missing audio or file permission

Imported files are copied locally. In Audio → My Files, open the folder you moved the track into; the root shows unfiled items. Search matches imported tracks across folders. If a new import fails, reselect the original in Files and check the provider app, network, free space, and supported format. Reset remembered file access in Settings only clears optional tokens; it does not delete your projects or grant new access.

Storage and exports

Clear preview cache to rebuild thumbnails. Clear Media Cache to remove duplicate import originals; project and audio-library copies remain. Finished utility files are in Tools → Exports. Compression attempts a smaller output, but already-efficient inputs may not shrink enough; review the actual result. Conversion and resizing serve a different purpose and may produce a larger file. Keep the app open during export.

Captions and privacy

Captions are optional. English uses Apple speech recognition, on-device when available; online fallback requires your explicit choice. Tamil uses a downloaded local model. Check every caption before sharing. The Privacy Policy explains network requests, storage, permissions, and deletion.
