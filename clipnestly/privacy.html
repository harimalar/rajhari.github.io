Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

ClipNestly is published by Hari Rajagopalan. Malar Hari is a creator/brand name, not a separate company or data provider.

Your working library of screenshots, saved images, text, links, recognized text, folders, favorites and preferences is stored on your iPhone. Apple's Vision framework recognizes text on the device. No ClipNestly account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated upload service is included. Optional iCloud Drive backup uses your Apple Account and is off by default.

Photos import uses Apple's picker. The app receives only your selections, not access to your entire photo library. By default, clipboard suggestions inspect only the available type and change count. Clipboard content is read when you tap Save or Paste. If you enable Auto-save Clipboard on Open, content is read when the app opens or returns to the foreground, subject to iOS paste permission. ClipNestly does not monitor the clipboard in the background. Its Share Extension receives only items you explicitly share. Content fingerprints prevent repeated imports and clipboard saves. They remain in the library and its backups and are not sent to the developer. Storage estimates and the last successful backup export date are kept locally. A separate local record remembers clipboard Save/dismiss choices using the clipboard change counter and elapsed time between app events; it contains no clipboard content, is excluded from backups, and is never sent off-device.

The app and Share Extension use a private App Group container. iOS file protection protects stored files while the device is locked. Your system device backup settings may include these files. ClipNestly does not sync its library between devices.

If you turn on Back up to iCloud Drive, ClipNestly writes a library backup, including images, text, links, recognized text, folders, favorites, history membership, pins, preferences and Recently Deleted items, into your iCloud Drive. Apple handles upload and storage under your iCloud settings. The developer does not receive these backups. Automatic updates run while ClipNestly is open, and iOS may finish uploading later. Turning this off stops future backups but does not remove existing files; delete them in Files if you want to remove them. Each installation updates its own backup file. An account change requires you to enable backup again. A local record of your backup choice, an opaque iCloud identity token, a random installation identifier, snapshot fingerprint and backup dates is excluded from backups and is not sent to the developer.

When you open a link, contact support, export a backup, or share an item, the destination application or service handles that information under its own policies. Exported backups contain your saved content and are not additionally encrypted by ClipNestly. Copies placed on the system clipboard can be read by destination apps subject to iOS permissions.

Deleted items remain in the working library’s Recently Deleted until you restore or permanently delete them. To remove a library copy, permanently delete it from Folders → Recently Deleted. Crop and redaction create a separate edited copy; the original stays in the library. Backups also include Recently Deleted items. Previously saved backups may retain items that were subsequently removed; manage those files in their destination. Clearing Clipboard History only removes history membership; saved items remain in Captures. Removing the app and its extension removes their local storage subject to iOS backup behavior. Original Photos images, iCloud backups and previously exported files remain in their destinations.
