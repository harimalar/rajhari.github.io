Privacy Policy

Your editing stays on your device. Optional network features and support are explained below.

Updated November 12, 2026

Email support: rajhari@gmail.com

Who is responsible

VideoForge Studio is owned and published by Hari Rajagopalan under the Malar Hari brand. Contact rajhari@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy covers the app, its share extension, our support correspondence, and the accompanying support website.

Editing and local information

Projects, imported photos, videos, audio, voice recordings, captions, edits, thumbnails, favorites, folders, export preferences, and finished exports are stored locally. We do not operate an editing server, require an app account, or automatically receive your projects or media. Imported files can retain embedded metadata such as dates, device details, or location. Review what you share. The app has no advertising, tracking SDK, or developer analytics SDK, and we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

Permissions are your choice

The system Photos picker provides the items you select. Saving an export to Photos asks for add permission. Apple Music access is used to list eligible local music you choose; protected subscription tracks cannot be imported. Microphone permission is requested for voiceover recording. Speech Recognition permission is requested for Apple speech transcription. You can decline these permissions and use other editing features. Change granted permissions in iOS Settings. Captions are optional and are not generated automatically.

Optional speech recognition

On-device Apple speech recognition is used when supported. When it is unavailable, the app requires you to explicitly enable online recognition before sending the selected speech audio to Apple. Apple processes that audio under its own privacy terms; recognition may require an internet connection. The online choice is off by default and resets when changing the caption language. Tamil captions use a local Whisper model. Choosing Download sends a request to Hugging Face and its delivery providers for that model; they receive network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Your recording is not sent to Hugging Face. The model is verified and transcription runs locally after download. Remove the model in the Tamil caption controls when it is no longer needed.

Files, cloud providers, and sharing

iOS Files provides access to enabled services such as pCloud, iCloud Drive, and NAS/SMB servers. You sign in with those services through iOS or their apps; VideoForge does not ask for their passwords or operate those accounts. The selected file is copied locally for offline editing. Optional file-access bookmarks are stored locally. Provider downloads, backups, and files you choose to export or share are handled under the destination service’s terms and privacy policy. We cannot revoke or delete copies held by those services.

Retention and deletion on your device

Deleting a project removes its project bundle. Deleting an audio-library item removes its library copy; projects that already use it keep their own copy. Separate duplicate originals can remain in MediaCache after either deletion or a failed import. Settings → Storage & Cache → Clear Media Cache removes those duplicate originals and their remembered access tokens. Clear preview cache removes rebuildable thumbnails and photo previews. Tools → Exports manages utility outputs separately. Remove files you saved to Photos or Files in those apps as well. Reset remembered file access removes stored bookmarks without deleting imported media. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to iOS; offloading the app can preserve documents. Device, iCloud, computer, or provider backups may retain copies until you manage or delete those backups. We cannot remotely erase your device or promise forensic erasure.

Support, diagnostics, and hosting

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, through our email provider (Google/Gmail). We use them to respond, investigate issues, and protect our legal rights, not for advertising. Keep sensitive footage, passwords, and unrelated personal information out of reports. The feedback button opens an email draft with app version/build; it does not automatically attach logs or media or send a message. We retain correspondence only as needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations; you may request deletion. Apple may process App Store purchases, device diagnostics, and developer reports under your Apple settings and its policies. The public support website is hosted with GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and request logs. Our static pages contain no advertising, analytics script, or tracking cookie added by us. Visiting external links subjects that visit to the destination’s policy.

Security and international services

The app uses iOS sandboxing and platform file protection for imported media; this is not a separate encrypted vault. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Protect your device and backups. Apple, cloud providers, GitHub, Hugging Face, and email providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own safeguards and policies.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete local content, clear caches, reset remembered access, remove the optional speech model, revoke permissions, decline online recognition, and choose whether to contact support. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to your local privacy authority. Email rajhari@gmail.com; we will respond as required by applicable law and request only information reasonably necessary to verify the request. We cannot access your local projects to retrieve or delete them for you. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights.

Children and changes

The app is a general-purpose editor, not a service directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information can contact us for deletion. Follow the App Store age rating and any local age or guardian-consent requirements. We will update this policy when practices change and identify the revision date. Material new data uses will be explained before they are enabled, with consent where required.
