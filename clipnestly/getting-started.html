Using ClipNestly

Save a good find

In Captures, tap + to choose up to 20 images from Photos. ClipNestly only receives the images you select. It cannot capture other apps’ screens itself; use the iPhone screenshot buttons, then import or share that screenshot.

Share from another app

Open the iOS share sheet, choose More, and enable ClipNestly. Share images, text or web links. Check the preview, choose Inbox or a folder, and tap Save. Return to ClipNestly to see the items. Text recognition runs in the main app. Reimporting identical content through Photos or Share reuses its saved item and keeps its existing folder, favorite and pin. Items in Recently Deleted are not revived by a new import.

Keep your clipboard useful

When you return to ClipNestly, a suggestion offers to save new clipboard content. Save takes the current image, text or web link. In Clipboard, tap Paste to save a copied image, text or link, or New Note to write your own. Enable Auto-save Clipboard on Open in Settings to save on return; iOS may request paste permission. Add Text or Link includes a Paste button and focused editor. Copies made inside ClipNestly appear in history when Remember Copies is enabled. Nothing is monitored in the background. Saving or dismissing a suggestion is remembered when the app relaunches, until the clipboard changes.

Find it again

Use All and Inbox in Captures, or open the Folders tab for your folders and Favorites. Images appear first as compact square tiles; text and links appear in slim rows below them. Tap + at the top right to import images, or the gear to open Settings. Use the sun or moon at the top left of Captures to switch between light and dark. Choose System in Settings to follow your iPhone’s appearance. Open an item for Copy Image, Copy Text, Copy Link, Share and filing. Pinch to zoom after tapping an image. Only http and https links are offered as web links; check recognized URLs before opening them.

Make it yours

The three tabs are Captures, Clipboard and Folders. Fresh launches open Clipboard for quick saves. Switching away and returning keeps your current tab and selection. In Folders, open Favorites or any custom folder to browse its captures. Tap New Folder to create one; touch and hold a folder to rename or delete it. Touch and hold a card and choose Select to start with that item selected, then tap more cards or Select All. You can also choose Move to Folder or Delete, or open an item and tap Move to Folder. You can create a folder while filing. Tap additional cards or Select All for batch filing, favorites and deletion. Selection actions appear above your cards; Done returns to browsing, and successful deletion also ends selection. Canceling a move keeps your selection. Folder deletion moves its items back to Inbox. Long-press Delete in Captures or Clipboard moves the item to Recently Deleted and offers Undo. Deleted items are available in Folders → Recently Deleted. Tap Undo to restore immediately, or restore later from Recently Deleted. Items stay there until permanently deleted. Swipe right on a Clipboard row, or touch and hold it, to pin a frequently reused item at the top.

Edit before sharing

Open saved text or a link, tap More, then Edit Text or Link. Save updates its content and detected links while keeping its folder, favorite and pin. Cancel leaves the saved version unchanged. Touch and hold a card or Clipboard row and choose Share to export it directly.

Open an image and tap Crop & Redact. Drag to select a crop or draw solid black covers over private details. Save Copy creates a separate, flattened image and recognizes text from the edited pixels. The original remains in your library; share the edited copy when you are ready.

Keep a backup

In Settings → Library & backup, turn on Back up to iCloud Drive to automatically update a backup after saves while ClipNestly is open. This is off by default. Back Up Now updates it immediately; iOS uploads it when iCloud Drive is available. Waiting for iCloud upload means the upload is not yet confirmed. Find the file in Files → iCloud Drive → ClipNestly → Backups. Each installation maintains its own backup file; newer backups replace that installation’s previous snapshot. Turning backup off leaves the file in iCloud Drive. An iCloud account change pauses backup until you enable it again. This does not synchronize the working library across devices.

Settings also shows library storage, the approximate space used by Recently Deleted, and the last successful manual export. Export Library Backup saves a JSON backup with embedded image data to a destination you choose. Canceling export does not update the backup date. Use Restore from Backup to select an iCloud or exported backup. Restore merges new items without replacing existing items or enabling automatic cloud backup. Backups support up to 150 MB of image data; larger libraries can export individual images through Share. Images are normalized, have source metadata removed, and are limited to 8,192 pixels on the longest side; keep Photos originals for archival quality.
