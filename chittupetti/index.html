CHITTUPETTI · சீட்டுப் பெட்டி

A little less paperwork.

A quiet receipt box for iPhone. Scan or import a receipt, check the details, and find it later by date.

Private by default

Receipt recognition runs on your device. Receipts stay in ChittuPetti’s local storage unless you choose to share or export them.

Made to put things away

ChittuPetti suggests a vendor, purchase date, and total, then organizes the PDF into year and month folders. Recognition can be wrong, so check details before relying on them.
