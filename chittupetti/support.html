To help us troubleshoot

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not email receipt images, payment information, passwords, or other sensitive details. Nothing is attached automatically.

Where receipts are saved

Receipts stay on your iPhone and are available in Files under On My iPhone → ChittuPetti → Receipts. ChittuPetti has no account or developer cloud sync. You can export or share a PDF or ZIP from within the app.

Recognition questions

Vendor, date, amount, and category suggestions are generated on-device and may need correction. You can edit the receipt details in the app.
