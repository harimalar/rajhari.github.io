Save. Organize. Reuse.

  • Import images from Photos, save from the share sheet, or paste text and links.
  • Copy words and detected links from screenshots using on-device text recognition.
  • Keep folders and favorites, pin useful clips, and edit a saved note.
  • Crop or cover private details in a separate edited image.
  • Recover deleted items and export a library backup.

Your working library stays local.

No ClipNestly account, ads or analytics SDK. Optional iCloud Drive backup uses your Apple Account and is off by default. Clipboard suggestions wait for your action; optional auto-save works when you open or return to the app. There is no background clipboard monitoring.

ClipNestly is independently usable and is a separate iPhone purchase from ScreenNestly for Mac. The apps do not sync.
