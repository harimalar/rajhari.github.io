Your records

Annam Care has no account or developer cloud backup. Food, water and fasting records stay on your device. Private records use an authenticated encrypted vault. The publisher cannot recover a lost vault key or retrieve local records.

Delete or control data

You → Data & Legal lets you erase food and water logs or turn widget sharing off. Manage fasting history in Fasting, saved plates and favorites in Food, and supplement intake in Herbs & Supplements. Erase private records from the unlocked Private tab. Disable Medical ID sharing and save to remove its locked-vault preview. Delete exported copies at their destinations.

Permissions

Manage microphone, speech recognition and notifications in iOS Settings; manage Health permissions in Apple Health. You can keep using typed food logging without these optional permissions.

Medical questions

Annam is a wellness journal, not medical advice or an emergency service. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions and local emergency services for emergencies.
