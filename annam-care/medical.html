About Annam Care

Annam Care is a lifestyle and wellness tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, is not a healthcare provider, and is not intended for use as a medical device. It does not replace professional medical care.

Before fasting or changing your diet

Consult a licensed physician or other qualified healthcare professional before starting a fasting regimen or changing your diet. Fasting may be unsuitable for children or adolescents, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for people with diabetes or a history of eating disorders, or for people taking medicines affected by food intake. Do not change medication or doses based on this app.

Goals and estimates

Default calorie, nutrient, water and fasting targets are placeholders, not personalized recommendations. Follow your care team’s advice, including any fluid restrictions. Food values are estimates and vary with portion size, ingredients and preparation. A completed fasting target is a time record, not a medical safety assessment.

Personal health records

The vault, imported readings and reports are for record keeping. Annam does not monitor emergencies or send clinical alerts. Its Medical ID is entered by the user and is not verified. Reminders may be delayed or unavailable. Keep original laboratory reports and prescriptions, and seek professional care when needed. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.
